DejaVu Sans 2.37

These unmodified TrueType fonts provide a subsetted fallback for Event PDFs.
Helvetica remains the primary font. Normal, bold, oblique and bold-oblique
faces are included to support Prawn's normal, bold, italic and bold_italic styles.

The four TTF files total approximately 2.7 MB (2.6 MiB). They are kept outside
the asset pipeline because they are read only by the server-side PDF renderer.
Generated PDFs embed only the required subsets, not all four complete fonts.
