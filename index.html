Renalware

Renalware is an open-source application for renal unit management. It brings together patient, clinical and pathology information to support kidney care, reporting and audit.

About these docs​

These docs bring together guidance for day-to-day use and the technical information needed to configure and support Renalware.

  • User Guide: registration, navigation, patient records, letters, and renal modules for clinical and other Renalware users.
  • Administration & Technical: reporting, patient nags, safety alerts, and test data for administrators, renal technicians, and developers.
  • Accessibility: Renalware's accessibility statement and how to report issues.
  • Onboarding videos: introductory training for new users.

Choose a section from the navigation above or search across all the documentation. Some technical guides assume familiarity with SQL and access to administrative tools.
